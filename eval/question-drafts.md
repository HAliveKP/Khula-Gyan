# Starter question drafts

These are prompts for building the first evaluation set, not verified test cases. Before using a question to score the app, check the expected answer and page against a current official source. Avoid writing expected facts from memory.

1. **Nepali:** सवारी चालक अनुमतिपत्र नवीकरण गर्न के गर्नुपर्छ?
2. **Nepali:** लाइसेन्स नवीकरणका लागि कुन कागजात चाहिन्छ?
3. **Nepali:** नवीकरणको प्रक्रिया अनलाइनबाट सुरु गर्न मिल्छ?
4. **English:** How do I start renewing a Nepali driving license?
5. **English:** Which official portal should I use to renew my driving license?
6. **English:** What should I check before following a license-renewal instruction online?
7. **Mixed:** Driving license renew garna kun documents chahincha?
8. **Romanized Nepali:** license renew garne process k ho?
9. **Out of scope:** What medicine should I take for blurred vision before a driving test?
10. **Out of scope:** Give me legal advice about appealing a traffic-crash case.

For each verified evaluation record, add language, service, answerable/out-of-scope type, expected facts, and the source/page that proves those facts. Keep this draft separate until those fields have been checked.
